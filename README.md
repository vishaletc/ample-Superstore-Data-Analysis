# Sample-Superstore-Data-Analysis
Superstore Sales Analysis using MySQL, Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Power BI
## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the **Sample Superstore dataset** using SQL, Python, and Power BI to identify meaningful business insights and understand sales and profitability performance.

The project covers the complete data analysis workflow, including **data extraction, cleaning, transformation, analysis, KPI calculation, data visualization, and interactive dashboard creation**.

The analysis helps answer important business questions related to sales, profit, orders, products, categories, sub-categories, regions, states, customer segments, and shipping modes.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Calculate important business KPIs.
* Identify the best-performing categories and sub-categories.
* Analyze sales performance across regions and states.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Compare customer segments based on sales and profit.
* Analyze shipping mode performance.
* Understand sales and profit trends over time.
* Create meaningful visualizations and an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate business insights from raw sales data.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis provides several important business insights:

* **Technology** generated the highest total profit among the major categories.
* **Consumer** generated the highest total sales among the customer segments.
* **Standard Class** generated the highest sales among shipping modes.
* **Phones** was the highest-selling sub-category by revenue.
* Product-level analysis helps identify high-revenue products for business focus.
* Profit margin analysis helps identify categories and segments with stronger profitability.

## 📌 Data Quality Note

During date-based analysis, the `Order_Date` field may require proper date formatting or conversion to a valid MySQL `DATE` or `DATETIME` data type before applying functions such as `YEAR()` and `DATE_FORMAT()`.

This ensures accurate monthly and yearly analysis.
